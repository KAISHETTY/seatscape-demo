# Seatscape demo

A 3D seat picker for movie theaters. Moviegoers can look around the auditorium in 360°, sit in any seat to see their view of the screen, and pick seats with the trailer playing on the big screen.

**Live demo:** https://kaishetty.github.io/seatscape-demo/

- Drag to look around, scroll or use +/- to zoom
- Click a seat to select it, double-click to sit in it
- Viewpoints: Entrance, Overview, From the screen, Sit in your seat

This is a demo with a sample showtime. No real tickets are sold.
